Our story

Meet the maker

Welcome to Eddie's Cakery Bar, where every cake is made with passion, creativity and a genuine love for celebrating life's special moments.

I'm Edwina, founder and CEO. What started as a passion for baking over six years ago has grown into a thriving cake business dedicated to creating beautiful, bespoke cakes and sweet treats for every occasion.

From birthdays and weddings to baby showers and corporate events, I believe every celebration deserves a cake as memorable as the moment itself. Each design is carefully crafted with attention to detail, quality ingredients and a personal touch that reflects your vision, looking stunning and tasting incredible too.

Over the years I've had the privilege of creating cakes for hundreds of celebrations, and there's nothing more rewarding than being part of such meaningful moments.

Made with love, designed with care, created to leave a lasting impression. ❤️
Edwina Omeleh, Founder & CEO, Eddie's Cakery Bar
